      Saving, explained

      Every change rewrites the page address (the bit after #art=). It holds your palette, layers, frames and title, run-length-encoded and base64url'd. Bookmark it, paste it in a chat, put it on a sticky note (a big sticky note). My saves keeps local copies with thumbnails in your browser's IndexedDB.

      Tips

      • Palette swap mode keeps colour numbers when you change palette, so you can instantly recolour a whole animation.
      • Onion skin shows the previous frame in red-ish ghosting and the next one fainter.
      • Long links can get truncated by some chat apps. If yours is over ~8 KB, also export a project file.
      • Rumour has it the console responds to a certain famous up-up-down-down sequence.
